Welcome to Stardust

A browser for people who make things. Set it up in a minute, or skip straight to browsing. You can change all of this later.

1 Bring your things over

Copy your bookmarks and history from another browser on this computer. Nothing is deleted from it.

1 Where do you post?

Pick the places you work in. They're added to your bookmarks so they're one click away.

2 Make your first workspace

A workspace keeps its own logins, tabs and cookies, so you can sign in to a few channels or brands side by side without mixing them up. Leave it empty to skip.

3 Good to know

    You can open this page again from the command bar: search "Welcome".

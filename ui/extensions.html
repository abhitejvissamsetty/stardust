Extensions

Add features to Stardust. Only add extensions from people you trust: they can see and change what you do on the sites you let them use.

Your changes take effect when Stardust restarts.

It will be able to:

      No extensions yet

      Add an extension you've downloaded, as an unpacked folder, a .zip file or a .crx file.

      Extensions that act on the tab you're looking at, such as page tools opened from their button, may not work yet. Content blockers, page changers and extensions with their own pages do.

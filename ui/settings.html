Workspaces

Each workspace keeps its own logins, cookies and open tabs, so you can stay signed in to a different account in each, such as one per channel. History, bookmarks, saved passwords and extensions are shared, and extensions only run in Personal. Deleting a workspace erases its logins and cookies the next time Stardust starts.
